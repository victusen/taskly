Data Protection

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This page explains the data protection practices behind Bzade: who is responsible for which data, the legal basis for using it, the safeguards we apply, how to exercise your rights, and how to have your data deleted. It supports our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, and follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who is responsible for what

Bzade as controller

For your account data (your name, email address, business name, sign in details, payment records and usage logs), Bzade decides why and how the data is used. Bzade is the data controller.

Bzade as processor

For the data you give Bzade to send your emails, meaning your recipient addresses, the names or other values read from your spreadsheet, and your message content, you decide why and how it is used. You are the controller and Bzade processes it only on your instructions, to store and send what you scheduled. You are responsible for having a lawful basis, such as consent or an existing customer relationship, to contact each recipient.

We have not appointed a separate Data Protection Officer. Requests and concerns go to the contact in section 11.

2. Data and the basis for using it

  • Account and sign in data. Needed to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • Mail connections, schedules and message content. Needed to send what you scheduled (contract and your instructions).
  • Payment records. Needed to take payment and meet tax and accounting duties (contract and legal obligation).
  • Security logs. Needed to protect accounts and the service (legitimate interest).
  • Optional features such as a Google Sheets connection run only after you choose to connect them (consent). You can withdraw consent by disconnecting.

3. Connected Google data

Gmail

Bzade uses the Gmail send permission only. We do not read, list, search or delete your messages. Bzade's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. During Google verification, the Gmail connection is available only to approved test users.

Google Sheets (planned)

When Bzade offers a Google Sheets connection, it will use read only access to the spreadsheet and the columns you choose, for example a column of email addresses and a column of names. Bzade will not write to, change or delete your spreadsheet. We will read only the columns you select, we will check and clean every value before it is stored or placed in an email, and we will update this page before the feature is released.

4. Safeguards

Access

  • Every request to our backend must carry a valid sign in token.
  • The backend always filters data by the signed in user, so one account cannot read another account's schedules or connections.
  • Database access rules are enabled so that browsers cannot read tables directly, except your own profile row.
  • Access to our hosting, database and provider dashboards is limited to the operator of Bzade.

Encryption

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • SMTP passwords and Google tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved. The key is kept in our hosting configuration, separate from the database.
  • Providers encrypt stored data at rest according to their own standards.

Input checking

  • Recipient addresses, subjects, message bodies, times and settings are checked in the browser and checked again on the server. The server check is the one that counts.
  • Values read from a spreadsheet, and the content of emails, are cleaned before they are stored or sent, so that scripts and unsafe links cannot be passed on to your recipients.
  • Requests are size limited and rate limited.

5. Providers and where data goes

These providers process data for Bzade, each for a specific purpose.

  • Supabase: sign in, database, payment start function.
  • Render: hosting for the Bzade backend and sending.
  • Cloudflare: domain name service, traffic protection, support address routing.
  • Paystack: payments. We receive the result, not your card details.
  • Google: Gmail sending, Google sign in and, when released, Google Sheets reading.
  • Your own mail provider: if you connect SMTP, that provider delivers the email under its own terms.
  • Transactional email provider: delivers Bzade account messages such as password resets.

These providers may store data outside Nigeria. We choose providers that publish security and data protection commitments, and we limit what each one receives. The full list with details is in the Privacy Policy.

6. Retention

  • Account, schedule and connection data stays until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Stored mail credentials are deleted when you disconnect that mail account.
  • Completed run history is kept so you can review it, and can be deleted by you.
  • After a deletion request we remove your data within 30 days. Backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
  • Payment records stay for as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and fault finding.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may:

  • ask whether we hold your data and receive a copy;
  • ask us to correct wrong data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • receive your data in a common format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Email victor@bzade.app from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We reply within 30 days.

If you are a recipient of an email sent through Bzade and want your data corrected or deleted, contact the business that sent it, because that business controls your data. You may also email us and we will pass your request on and act on any instruction from that business.

8. Delete your data

You can have your data deleted in either of these ways.

  • Disconnect a mail account from the Bzade dashboard. This deletes the stored credentials for that account.
  • Delete your whole account. Email victor@bzade.app with the subject "Delete my Bzade account" from your account email. We delete your profile, schedules, connections and run history within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you signed in with Google, Facebook or X, also remove Bzade from that provider's list of connected apps. For Google, open your Google Account, choose Security, then Third party apps with account access, and remove Bzade. Removing the app at the provider stops further access, and our deletion request removes the data we hold.

9. Personal data breaches

If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware, as the NDPA requires, and we will tell affected users without undue delay, with what happened, what data was involved, and what to do.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can fix the problem. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.
