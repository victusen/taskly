Data Protection

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Bzade helps businesses prepare and schedule individual emails through Gmail or SMTP, with optional Google Sheets personalization. This page explains how responsibilities and data access work. Read it with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

1. Who is responsible for what

Bzade as controller

Bzade uses account, project, security and payment records to operate the service, manage subscriptions and protect accounts. For these records, Bzade determines the purposes and means of processing.

Bzade as processor

For recipient addresses, spreadsheet values and message content that you provide, your business decides whom to contact and why. Bzade processes that data to prepare and send the messages you request. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to contact each recipient and for honoring their privacy rights.

Contact details for privacy requests are in section 11.

2. Data and the basis for using it

  • Account and project data. Used to provide your account and workspace.
  • Mail connections, schedules and message data. Used to prepare, personalize and send emails at your instruction.
  • Google Sheets data. Used only when you connect Sheets and select a spreadsheet and columns for a schedule.
  • Payment records. Used to manage subscriptions and meet applicable accounting or legal duties.
  • Feedback, invitations and technical logs. Used to respond to you, manage access and protect or troubleshoot the service.

The lawful basis depends on the data and feature involved. It may include providing the service you request, your instructions or choices, legal duties, and protecting the service.

3. Connected Google data

Gmail

The Gmail connection requests the gmail.send permission. It lets Bzade send messages from your account; it does not let Bzade read, list, search, delete or download your Gmail. We use Google data only to provide the feature you choose. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. The Bzade operator does not routinely review it, but may access it when necessary for support, security, abuse investigation or legal compliance. Our use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Google Sheets

The separate Sheets connection requests Google's drive.file permission for files you create or select for use with Bzade. Bzade reads the selected spreadsheet's headers, recipient addresses and personalization columns when preparing a send. It does not update the source rows. If you ask Bzade to create a spreadsheet, it creates the file and writes the header row. You can disconnect Sheets in Bzade or revoke access in your Google Account.

4. Safeguards

Access

  • Account and project operations require sign-in and are scoped to the signed-in user.
  • Backend inputs are validated and request sizes are limited.
  • The Bzade operator does not routinely review message or recipient data.

Encryption

  • Connections to Bzade use HTTPS.
  • SMTP passwords and Google OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. The key is held in the backend hosting configuration, separately from the database.
  • Message content, recipient data and schedules are not encrypted by Bzade with a separate application-level key.

Input checking

  • Schedule fields and spreadsheet values are validated by the backend before processing.
  • Emails are prepared and sent individually to each To address. Cc and Bcc recipients are included as entered.

5. Providers and where data goes

Providers process data under their own privacy notices and terms.

  • Supabase: authentication, database and payment initialization. Supabase Auth sends account emails through its configured email service.
  • Render: backend hosting and scheduled email processing.
  • Cloudflare: domain and network services.
  • Paystack: payment processing. Bzade receives payment references and status, not full card details.
  • Google, GitHub, Facebook and X: optional sign-in. Google also provides Gmail sending and Google Sheets access.
  • Your own mail provider: if you connect SMTP, that provider delivers the email under its own terms.

Providers may store or process data outside Nigeria. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

6. Retention

  • Deleting a schedule stops future sends but retains its record. Deleting a project marks it deleted; associated records may remain stored.
  • Disconnecting an email or Sheets connection removes its stored credentials.
  • Account deletion is handled by request. Email us to ask; there is no fixed completion time. Some records may need to remain for legal, security or service reasons.
  • Payment records may be retained for accounting, legal and dispute-resolution purposes. Technical logs may be retained under provider operational policies.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may:

  • ask whether we hold your data and receive a copy;
  • ask us to correct wrong data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • receive your data in a common format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Email Victor@bzade.app. We may ask you to confirm your identity before responding.

If you received an email sent through Bzade and want your data corrected or deleted, contact the business that sent it. That business decides how your recipient data is used.

8. Delete your data

You can make these requests:

  • Disconnect a mail or Sheets account in Bzade. This removes its stored credentials.
  • Request account deletion. Email Victor@bzade.app from your account email. We will review the request; completion time depends on verification and any records we must retain.

You may also revoke Bzade's access in your Google Account. Open your Google Account, choose Security, then Third party apps with account access, and remove Bzade. Revoking access stops future Google access; it does not delete records already held by Bzade.

9. Personal data breaches

We will notify affected people and regulators when required by applicable law.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can fix the problem. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.
