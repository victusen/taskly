What counts as a send & request

One recipient is one send. One API call is one request. We count exact units with zero hidden overhead.

What happens at the limit

Sending is paused until the next month or your upgrade. The API returns a clear 429 status, not silent failures.

What happens when a plan expires

Existing schedules keep running for a grace period. New creation is blocked, matching the database trigger.

Cancel anytime

No card lock-in and no hidden cancellation fees. Payload, SMS and WhatsApp channels are labeled but not priced.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common billing questions

Billing is processed in USD or local currency conversion depending on your payment location through our secure payment gateway. All amounts are transparently displayed before confirmation.
If you cancel within the first 7 days of subscribing and have not exceeded fair usage quotas, reach out to our team for a prompt refund. You can cancel future renewals anytime with a single click.
One recipient equals one send. If a single schedule delivers an email to 10 recipients, it counts as 10 sends. Each REST API call made to create, update, or trigger a schedule counts as one API request.
Business and Custom plans allow project owners to invite collaborators with granular roles (viewer, editor, admin) to manage campaign templates without sharing mailbox passwords.
When connecting Gmail accounts, Google imposes daily limits (typically 500 emails/day for free Gmail, 2,000 emails/day for Google Workspace). Custom SMTP connections use the limits defined by your SMTP host.
If a subscription expires or is canceled, your existing schedules enter a grace period so ongoing operations are not immediately disrupted. New schedule creation is paused until reactivated.
