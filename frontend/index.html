Meet Zade

She runs a busy shop, wearing every hat

Zade manages a restaurant with a small team. Between managing staff, sourcing supplies, and serving customers, every hour counts. Communication with regulars too often falls through the cracks.

The Problem

Customer data, buried in spreadsheets

Zade keeps a running Excel file of loyal customers. Birthdays, preferences, last orders. All there. But sending a timely email to 80 people means opening a laptop, composing manually, and hoping nothing breaks.

The Intent

Warm interactions, she kept close

Zade knows that a well-timed message, a birthday note, a weekly special, a quiet thank-you, keeps customers coming back. The relationship is there. The delivery system is not.

The Solution

Zade signs up to Bzade.app

In under 3 minutes, she connects her Gmail account, uploads the customer list, and sets a weekly schedule. every Friday, her customers get a personal message automatically, on time, every time.

Answers to common billing questions

Billing is handled in both US Dollars and Nigerian Naira (NGN). Payments made via card are charged at the exact subscription fee (e.g., $2.99), while payments via bank transfer are charged at the rounded figure (e.g., $3 to be transferred) since card deductions allow exact decimals.
If you cancel within the first 7 days of subscribing and have not exceeded fair usage quotas, reach out to our team for a prompt refund. You can cancel future renewals anytime with a single click.
Business and Custom plans allow project owners to invite collaborators with granular roles (viewer, editor, admin) to manage campaign templates without sharing mailbox passwords.
When connecting Gmail accounts, Google imposes daily limits (typically 500 emails/day for free Gmail, 2,000 emails/day for Google Workspace). Custom SMTP connections use the limits defined by your SMTP host.
If a subscription expires or is canceled, your existing schedules enter a grace period so ongoing operations are not immediately disrupted. New schedule creation is paused until reactivated. You can also opt to immediately seize all on the go directly from your dashboard.
Sending is paused until the next billing month or your upgrade. The API returns a clear 429 status code rather than a silent failure, so you always know exactly what happened.
We are running this rates for every paying user in their first year, this is to help them build, scale and automate their marketing workflow very affordably.
Yes. No card lock-in and no hidden cancellation fees. Cancel with a single click from your billing settings. Payload, SMS, and WhatsApp channels are labeled on the roadmap but are not yet priced. Thanks for checking our FAQ.
