Bzade.app

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Bzade ("Bzade", "we", "us", "our") is a service that lets business owners write emails and schedule them to be sent from an email account the owner connects. Bzade is operated by Victor Usen from Nigeria.

This policy explains what personal data Bzade collects, why, which outside providers help us run the service, how long we keep data, and the choices you have. It applies to the Bzade website, the Bzade dashboard and the Bzade backend services. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Data we collect

Account data

  • Your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and we never see or store them in readable form.
  • If you sign in with Google: the basic profile that Google shares for sign in, such as your email address.
  • Business or profile details you choose to enter.

Mail connection data

To send email for you, you connect a mail account in one of two ways.

Gmail connection

  • Your Gmail address.
  • An access token and a refresh token that Google issues to Bzade after you approve the connection. These let Bzade send mail as you without knowing your Google password.
  • The permission granted, which is limited to sending email (the gmail.send permission).

Custom SMTP connection

  • Your sender address, SMTP server name, port, security setting and username.
  • Your SMTP password or key. We store it encrypted, as described in section 7.

Schedule and message data

  • The recipients you enter, the subject, the message text, the date, time and time zone, and the repeat setting.
  • The status of each schedule: waiting, sent, paused, cancelled or failed, with the time of the last attempt and the reason for any failure.

Recipient addresses belong to other people. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to contact them, as explained in our Terms of Use.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Paystack. We receive the payment reference, the status, the amount, the currency and the email address used for the payment. We never receive or store your full card number, card PIN or card security code.

Technical data

Our servers and providers record IP address, browser type, request time and error details so that we can keep the service secure and fix faults.

2. Why we use your data

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To send the emails you schedule, at the time you choose, from the account you connected.
  • To show your schedules and their status in your dashboard.
  • To process payments and keep payment records.
  • To send you account messages such as password resets and security notices.
  • To detect abuse, protect the service and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your messages, recipient lists or connected account data for advertising. Bzade staff do not read the content of your emails. Message content is stored and processed by our systems because that is how a scheduled email is sent, and we may access it only where needed to investigate abuse, fix a fault you report, or comply with the law.

3. Providers that process data for Bzade

We use the following providers. Each receives only the data it needs for its role.

Supabase (accounts and database)

Supabase provides sign in, password handling, the database that stores your account, mail connections and schedules, and the serverless function that starts a payment. Your schedule content and encrypted credentials are stored in this database.

Render (application hosting)

Render hosts the Bzade backend that checks your requests, decrypts your mail credentials at the moment of sending, and delivers your scheduled emails. Request logs may pass through Render.

Cloudflare (network and email routing)

Cloudflare provides domain name service, traffic protection and the routing of mail sent to Bzade addresses such as our support address. Traffic to bzade.app and api.bzade.app passes through Cloudflare.

Paystack (payments)

When you pay, Paystack collects your card or bank details on its own checkout and acts as the payment processor. Paystack handles that data under its own privacy policy. We receive the result of the payment, not your card details.

Google (Gmail connection and Google sign in)

If you connect Gmail, Google authenticates you and issues the tokens described above, and Google delivers the emails Bzade sends through the Gmail service. Section 4 describes our commitments for Google data.

The mail provider you choose (custom SMTP)

If you connect a custom SMTP account, for example from Hostinger, Zoho Mail, Brevo, Resend or your own mail server, Bzade connects to that server using the details you entered and hands it each email to deliver. That provider processes the email under your agreement with it and its own privacy policy. Bzade does not control it.

Email delivery for Bzade account messages

Password reset and sign up messages are sent to you through a transactional email provider that we configure in Supabase (such as Resend or Brevo). It receives your email address and the message content for that purpose only.

Content delivery

Our pages load some files from third party networks: the Supabase script from jsDelivr, fonts from Google Fonts and the checkout script from Paystack. These networks may see your IP address when your browser requests those files.

If we add another provider, for example another sending channel such as Telegram, we will update this section before the provider receives your data.

4. Google user data

Bzade requests only the Gmail permission needed to send email (gmail.send). We use it for one purpose: to send the emails you schedule from your Gmail account. We do not read, list, search, delete or download any message in your mailbox.

Bzade's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide the sending feature you asked for.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to send your email, to follow the law, or as part of a sale of the service with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or to build advertising profiles.
  • No person at Bzade reads your Google data unless you give consent for a specific item, it is needed to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

While Bzade completes Google verification, the Gmail connection is available only to approved test users. Google may ask test users to reconnect every seven days.

You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Bzade, and you can also remove Bzade's access in your Google Account under Security, then Third party apps with account access.

5. Sending behavior

  • An email is sent only from a mail account you connected and only to the recipients you entered.
  • Bzade sends at the time and time zone you set. If our service is unavailable at that moment, the email may be sent late or may fail, and a failure is shown on your dashboard.
  • Emails sent through Gmail or your SMTP provider also appear in that provider's own records, such as your Sent folder, and are subject to that provider's policies.

6. Sharing

We share personal data only with the providers listed in section 3, with authorities where the law requires it, with professional advisers under confidentiality, and with a buyer if Bzade is sold, in which case we will notify you.

7. Security

  • All traffic between your browser, Bzade and our providers uses HTTPS.
  • Mail credentials, meaning SMTP passwords and Google tokens, are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved. The encryption key is held in our hosting configuration and not in the database.
  • Every request to the backend must carry a valid sign in token, and each user can only reach their own schedules and connections.
  • The backend limits request sizes and validates all input before storing it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where the law requires it.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account, schedule and connection data: until you delete it or your account.
  • Disconnecting a mail account deletes the stored credentials for it.
  • After you request account deletion, we delete your account data within 30 days. Backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
  • Payment records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and fault finding.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • give you your data in a usable format.

Email us at the address in section 13. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

10. Storage in other countries

Our providers run servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside Nigeria. We choose providers that apply security and contract protections for transferred data.

11. Cookies and browser storage

Bzade keeps your sign in session in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies.

12. Age and changes

Bzade is for people aged 18 and over. We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and we will tell you about significant changes by email or in the dashboard.
