How our pricing works

Clear rules with zero surprises

We believe businesses should never be punished for growing their subscriber lists. Here is exactly how our billing and resource quotas work.

Exact send counting

One recipient equals one send unit. If a schedule delivers to 10 customers, it counts as 10 sends. There is zero hidden multiplier overhead or per-contact database storage penalty.

Direct inbox quota limits

When connecting Gmail, Google Workspace guidelines apply (typically 500/day for free Gmail and 2,000/day for Workspace). For custom SMTP, your mail server provider's quota dictates sending ceilings.

Predictable 429 overage safety

If your plan limit is reached, transmission is paused with explicit notifications. Our API returns an actionable 429 status code rather than quietly failing or billing surprise surge charges.

7-day money-back refund

If you cancel within 7 days of subscribing and have not exceeded fair use thresholds, our team issues a prompt, hassle-free refund with zero questions asked.

Cancel anytime in 1 click

No long-term contracts or lock-in hoops. You can pause or cancel your subscription anytime directly from your dashboard billing settings without contacting sales.

Grace period data protection

If a plan expires, ongoing schedules enter an operational grace window so critical customer appointments and notifications are not instantly cut off.
