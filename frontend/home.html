How it works

From sign up to first send in three steps

  1. 1

    Create a project

    Add your project name and business name. This is where all your scheduled emails live.

  2. 2

    Connect your email

    Link your Gmail or your own SMTP account. Customers see your address, not ours.

  3. 3

    Write and schedule

    Pick the recipients, write the message, choose when and how often. Bzade does the rest.
