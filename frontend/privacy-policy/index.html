Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Bzade ("Bzade", "we", "us", "our") helps businesses prepare and schedule individual emails through a connected Gmail or SMTP account. You can also use Google Sheets to select recipients and personalize messages. Bzade is operated by Victor Usen from Nigeria.

This policy explains the data we process, why we process it, which providers help operate Bzade, and how to make a privacy request. It applies to the Bzade website, dashboard and backend.

1. Data we collect

Account and workspace data

  • Your email address, sign-in details, and profile information such as your name.
  • If you sign in with Google, GitHub, Facebook or X, the account details that provider shares with Supabase.
  • Project and business names, workspace settings, and invitation details you provide.

Connected accounts and email data

  • Gmail or Google Sheets account identifiers and OAuth tokens you authorize.
  • SMTP server settings, sender address, username, and password or app key.
  • Email addresses in To, Cc and Bcc; subject lines; message content; schedule settings; and delivery status.
  • When you connect Google Sheets, the spreadsheet and columns you select, including recipient addresses and personalization values.

Spreadsheet values are read when needed to prepare a scheduled send. Bzade processes recipient data to create and send the individual messages you request.

Payments, feedback and technical data

Paystack processes payments. Bzade receives payment references, status, amount and currency for subscription records. We do not receive your full card number, PIN or security code. If you send feedback, we store its message, category, optional page address and account identifier. We also store invitation addresses, roles and notes that you submit.

Our service providers may record technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times and error logs.

2. Why we use your data

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To store and manage projects, schedules and connected accounts.
  • To read selected spreadsheet values, personalize messages and send each email as instructed.
  • To process payments and keep payment records.
  • To send you account messages such as password resets and security notices.
  • To detect abuse, protect the service and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We do not use connected email or Google data to train AI or machine-learning models. Bzade does not routinely review message content or recipient lists. Our systems process them to prepare and send emails. The Bzade operator may access stored data when necessary for support, security, abuse investigation or legal compliance.

3. Providers that process data for Bzade

We use the following providers. Each receives only the data it needs for its role.

Supabase (accounts and database)

Supabase provides authentication, the database, and payment initialization. It stores account, project, schedule and connection records. Account messages such as sign-in and password-reset emails are sent through Supabase Auth's configured email service.

Render (application hosting)

Render hosts the backend that checks requests and sends scheduled emails. SMTP and Google credentials are decrypted server-side when needed.

Cloudflare (domain and network services)

Cloudflare provides domain and network services for Bzade.

Paystack (payments)

When you pay, Paystack collects your card or bank details on its own checkout and acts as the payment processor. Paystack handles that data under its own privacy policy. We receive the result of the payment, not your card details.

Google (sign-in, Gmail and Sheets)

Google provides optional sign-in, Gmail sending and Google Sheets access. Google delivers messages sent through Gmail. See section 4 for the access each connection requests.

GitHub, Facebook and X (optional sign-in)

If you choose one of these providers to sign in, Supabase uses it to authenticate you and receives the account details the provider shares.

The mail provider you choose (custom SMTP)

If you connect a custom SMTP account, for example from Hostinger, Zoho Mail, Brevo, Resend or your own mail server, Bzade connects to that server using the details you entered and hands it each email to deliver. That provider processes the email under your agreement with it and its own privacy policy. Bzade does not control it.

Content delivery

Our pages load some files from third party networks: the Supabase script from jsDelivr, fonts from Google Fonts and the checkout script from Paystack. These networks may see your IP address when your browser requests those files.

Your connected SMTP provider receives the email content and recipient details needed to deliver your messages. Providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Google user data

The Gmail connection requests Google's send-email permission (gmail.send). It lets Bzade send messages from your account; it does not let Bzade read, list, search, delete or download your Gmail. Google Sheets is a separate connection. It requests file access (drive.file) for spreadsheets you create or select for use with Bzade. Bzade reads the selected header, recipient and personalization columns. If you ask Bzade to create a spreadsheet, it creates the file and writes the header row.

Bzade's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google data only to provide the account, sending or spreadsheet feature you choose.
  • We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • We share it only with providers needed to operate the feature, or when law requires it.
  • The Bzade operator does not routinely review Google data, but may access it when necessary for support, security, abuse investigation or legal compliance.

You can disconnect either connection in Bzade. You can also revoke access in your Google Account settings.

5. Sending behavior

  • An email is sent only from a mail account you connected and only to the recipients you entered.
  • Each To recipient receives a separate message. Cc and Bcc recipients you add are included with each message.
  • For spreadsheet schedules, Bzade reads the selected rows and columns when preparing each send. It does not update the source rows.
  • Bzade sends at the time and time zone you set. If our service is unavailable at that moment, the email may be sent late or may fail, and a failure is shown on your dashboard.
  • Emails sent through Gmail or your SMTP provider also appear in that provider's own records, such as your Sent folder, and are subject to that provider's policies.

6. Sharing

We share personal data only with the providers listed in section 3, with authorities where the law requires it, with professional advisers under confidentiality, and with a buyer if Bzade is sold, in which case we will notify you.

7. Security

  • Connections to Bzade use HTTPS.
  • SMTP passwords and Google OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. The encryption key is held in the backend hosting configuration, separately from the database.
  • Authenticated account and project operations require a valid sign-in token and are scoped to the signed-in user.
  • The backend validates inputs and limits request sizes.

Message content, recipient data and schedules are not encrypted by Bzade with a separate application-level key. No system is perfectly secure. We will notify people and authorities when required by law.

8. How long we keep data

  • Deleting a schedule stops future sends but retains its record. Deleting a project marks it deleted; associated records may remain stored.
  • Disconnecting an email or Sheets connection removes its stored credentials.
  • Account deletion is handled by request. Contact us for review; we do not promise a fixed completion time. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or service reasons.
  • Payment records may be retained for accounting, legal and dispute-resolution purposes. Technical logs may be retained by Bzade and its providers under their operational policies.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • give you your data in a usable format.

Email us at the address in section 13. We may ask you to verify your identity. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

10. Storage in other countries

Our providers may store or process data outside Nigeria. Their privacy notices describe their locations and practices. We take steps required by applicable data protection law.

11. Cookies and browser storage

Bzade uses browser storage for your sign-in session, saved drafts, profile image and preferences. These items remain on your device unless you clear them or remove them in Bzade. We do not use personal data for advertising.

12. Age and changes

Bzade is for people aged 18 and over. We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and we will tell you about significant changes by email or in the dashboard.
