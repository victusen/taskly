Bzade.app

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Bzade.app ("Bzade", "we", "us", or "our") provides software that helps users create, schedule, manage, and send email communications through email accounts and services that the user chooses to connect.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bzade collects, why we collect it, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Bzade, we may collect:

Account information

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication information provided through our authentication provider
  • Business or organization information that you choose to provide

Email connection information

If you connect an email account to Bzade, we may process information necessary to send emails on your behalf.

Custom SMTP connections

  • Sender email address
  • SMTP server address
  • SMTP port
  • SMTP username
  • SMTP security settings
  • SMTP authentication credentials

SMTP passwords and other sensitive credentials are encrypted before they are stored by Bzade.

Google connections

For Google connections, Bzade may receive and securely store OAuth credentials necessary to send email on your behalf, including a refresh token and related token information.

Bzade does not request or store your Google account password.

Email and scheduling information

When you use Bzade to create and schedule messages, we may process:

  • Recipient email addresses
  • Sender information
  • Email subject
  • Email content
  • Email templates
  • Scheduled delivery times
  • Delivery status
  • Error and retry information

Payment information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Paystack.

Bzade does not need to store your complete card details when those details are processed directly by the payment provider.

Technical information

We may collect information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Application logs
  • Authentication events
  • Error logs
  • Security events

2. Why We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and maintain your Bzade account
  • Authenticate you
  • Provide Bzade's email and scheduling features
  • Send emails at your instruction
  • Store and manage email templates
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Maintain application security
  • Communicate important service information
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your connected mailbox credentials for unrelated purposes.

3. Google Account Connections

When you choose to connect Gmail, Bzade uses Google's OAuth authorization system.

Google presents you with the permissions Bzade requests before access is granted.

For Bzade's current Gmail sending feature, Bzade requests permission to send email on your behalf.

Bzade does not receive your Google password.

Google authentication credentials received by Bzade are handled by our backend and are not intentionally exposed to your browser.

You can revoke Bzade's access through your Google Account settings.

4. Custom SMTP Connections

If you connect a custom SMTP mailbox, you provide the SMTP information required to send email through that mailbox.

Bzade encrypts sensitive SMTP credentials before storing them.

Only the server-side systems responsible for providing the email service should be able to decrypt and use those credentials.

You should only connect a mailbox that you are authorized to use.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

  • HTTPS/TLS communication
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Server-side credential handling
  • Encryption of stored email credentials
  • Restricted access to production secrets
  • Database access controls
  • Logging and security monitoring
  • Input validation
  • Rate limiting and abuse controls
  • Secure third-party authentication flows

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

Bzade may use trusted third-party providers to operate specific parts of the service.

  • Supabase for authentication and database infrastructure
  • Google for Gmail authorization and Gmail API services
  • Yahoo where Yahoo email integration is supported
  • Paystack for payment processing
  • Cloudflare for storage or asset delivery

7. Email Content

Bzade processes email content because that processing is necessary to provide features that you explicitly request, including creating, scheduling, and sending emails.

Bzade does not sell your email content.

Bzade does not intentionally read or inspect your email content for advertising purposes.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or maintain legitimate business records.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Know how your personal information is processed
  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request portability of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the applicable data protection authority

10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Bzade account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Where technically and legally possible, we will delete information that is no longer required.

11. Children

Bzade is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the service under applicable law.

12. International Processing

Bzade and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country in which you live.

13. Cookies and Local Storage

Bzade may use local storage or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, and application functionality.

Where non-essential technologies require consent under applicable law, Bzade will request that consent where required.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal obligations, or privacy practices change.

The updated version will display a new effective date.
